### 👋 About

I build and run Google Cloud and Workspace systems for businesses across North America and Latin America, from El Salvador. Through my consultancy, **[Guanacos Tech](https://guanacostech.com)**, I have shipped 80+ engagements since 2018: migrations, architecture reviews, email deliverability rescues, and Gemini-powered automations that quietly do their job.

- 🏗️ **Cloud architecture** on GCP: Cloud Run, GKE, BigQuery, Firebase, Vertex AI
- 📬 **Email deliverability**: SPF, DKIM, DMARC, BIMI, warm-up, Instantly / GoHighLevel / Apollo pipelines
- 🔁 **Workspace migrations**: M365, IMAP, Zoho and legacy hosts into Google Workspace, zero-downtime cutovers
- 🤖 **AI automation**: Gemini Enterprise, ADK agents, Apps Script, Claude-driven tooling
- 🛡️ **Security**: studying Cybersecurity at ESIT, running phishing-kit takedowns and incident response for clients
- 🌎 **Bilingual** EN / ES, same response time in both, across NA and LATAM time zones
